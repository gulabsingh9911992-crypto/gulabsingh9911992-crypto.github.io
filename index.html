PeakCut

PeakCut is a personal software tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel "Gamer Inside". It records live streams that the owner selects, cuts short highlight clips from them, and uploads those clips to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

PeakCut is used by a single user — the channel owner himself. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's data is accessed or processed.
