Privacy Policy for PeakCut

Last updated: 20 November 2026

PeakCut is a personal tool operated by a single user (the owner of the YouTube channel "Gamer Inside") to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

Through Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API, PeakCut accesses only the owner's own Google account information needed to identify the channel, and uploads video files from the owner's own computer to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How data is used

The data is used solely to upload the owner's videos and to display the owner's own channel information inside the tool. No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. No data is used for advertising.

Data storage

OAuth tokens and all data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on external servers.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke PeakCut's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately stops the tool from accessing the account.
